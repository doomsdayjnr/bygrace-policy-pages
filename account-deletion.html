Account deletion

By Grace · AGA Productions · Updated 29 September 2026

Email an account deletion request

Request deletion of your By Grace account

Email agaproductions77@gmail.com with the subject By Grace account deletion request. If you can sign in, you can also open Profile > Delete account to review what is affected, or use Profile > Contact us to request help. You can use the email route even after uninstalling the app. Never send your password, a one-time code or identity documents in the first message.

Self-service deletion is currently unavailable while release safeguards are being completed. A support request does not itself delete the account. We will explain how to confirm account ownership safely and what remains to complete the request. Do not use another project's deletion page for By Grace.

What happens to your data

An account owner must first transfer any owned groups to an adult who accepts or explicitly close them. Deletion removes the account, profile, its own posts and sent private messages, and ends guardian links. Other participants keep their own messages in a closed conversation. Saved reports and moderation evidence may remain for staff safety review, including reported text or a saved display name. Open reviews, safety holds, provider backups and operational logs may require separate handling.
