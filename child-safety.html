Child safety standards

By Grace · AGA Productions · Updated 29 September 2026

Our standard

By Grace prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including grooming, sexual coercion, sextortion, trafficking for sexual exploitation, attempts to obtain sexual content from a child, and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Members must not use the app to arrange, encourage, share or conceal such conduct. Accounts are for people aged 13 and older; a guardian's approval is required before a minor joins any group. Guardian approval does not verify another person's identity or make contact safe.

Report a concern in the app

Use Report on a concerning post, group or message. Reporting a private message gives authorized reviewers the specific reported text, not general access to the conversation. You can also use Profile > Contact us to send a private safety ticket. If you cannot access the app, email agaproductions77@gmail.com with the subject By Grace child safety concern. Describe where the concern occurred and how staff can find it. Do not forward or attach suspected illegal child sexual abuse material.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate child-protection authority. By Grace support is not an emergency service.

How we respond

Authorized moderators and admins review reports and may restrict or remove content or accounts under the Community guidelines. Suspected CSAM or exploitation is escalated for safety review and handled under applicable law, including reports to appropriate authorities when required. We preserve relevant report evidence for authorized staff review under the retention rules in our Privacy policy. We do not promise around-the-clock monitoring or automatic detection of every harmful interaction.

Contact

AGA Productions operates By Grace. For child-safety questions or reports, use the in-app Report or Contact us actions, or email agaproductions77@gmail.com. Google Play's designated child-safety contact is provided separately in Play Console.
