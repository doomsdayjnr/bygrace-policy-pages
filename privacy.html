Privacy policy

By Grace · AGA Productions · Updated 29 September 2026

Who runs By Grace

By Grace is operated under the name AGA Productions. This policy describes the By Grace mobile app and its support services. For a privacy question, use Contact us in the app or email agaproductions77@gmail.com. Do not send a password by email.

Information used by the app

We use your account email and sign-in details to create and protect your account. We store the display name, optional real name, date of birth, selected profile image and preferences you provide. Date of birth is used to enforce the 13-and-older rule and youth safeguards. Authentication is provided by Supabase Auth.

We store what you choose to do in the app, including prayer requests and reactions, devotionals and comments, group details and membership, meetings and RSVPs, discussions, private messages, support tickets, reports, appeals and safety decisions. Your words may include beliefs, health concerns or information about others if you choose to include them. Please share only what is necessary and do not post someone else's private information without permission.

The app stores an account identifier, notification and follow preferences, and the records needed to deliver these features. Sign-in and some feed preferences are stored on your device. Service providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure the app.

Who can see information

Your public profile and content you publish to community areas may be seen by other eligible members. Group discussions are for members with access to that group. Private messages are for the conversation participants; staff do not receive the whole conversation merely because someone reports a message.

A report may give authorized moderators and admins a saved copy of the specific reported content and your explanation. Staff can also see support requests and records needed for safety review. A guardian can review protected group and chat requests involving a linked minor. Guardian approval does not verify another person's identity or suitability.

Why information is used and where it goes

We use information to run accounts and community features, enforce age and guardian protections, deliver in-app alerts, respond to support, prevent misuse, investigate reports and comply with applicable obligations. We use Supabase to host authentication, database and app backend services. Information is shared with other members only as described above or when you choose to publish or send it. We do not sell personal information. Ads and paid membership purchases are not active in this version.

Security and retention

We use authenticated access and database access rules to limit who can read private records, and encrypted connections when data is sent to the service. No system can guarantee perfect security.

Account and activity records are kept while needed to provide the service and until a valid deletion or applicable content-removal process completes. Closed support cases become eligible for reviewed cleanup after 90 days. Resolved moderation cases and saved evidence become eligible after 12 months. Open cases, appeals, active safety holds and records with unknown dates can be kept longer. Cleanup is reviewed manually; these periods are eligibility points, not promises that every copy disappears automatically on that day. Provider backups and operational logs may follow separate schedules.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your display name and profile image, change available preferences, remove some of your content, leave groups where permitted, block members and contact support. You can review account deletion from Profile > Delete account. Self-service deletion is currently unavailable while release safeguards are being completed; email agaproductions77@gmail.com with the subject By Grace account deletion request or use Contact us if you can sign in. We will explain the identity-check and next steps without asking for your password by email.

Before an owned group can be removed with your account, its ownership must be transferred to an adult who accepts or the group must be explicitly closed. Account deletion removes your profile and your own posts and private messages. Another participant may keep their own messages in a closed conversation. Saved reports and moderation evidence may remain accessible to authorized staff for safety review, including reported text or a saved display name. See the account deletion page for the request route and more detail.
